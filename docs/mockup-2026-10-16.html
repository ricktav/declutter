What the numbers mean

  1. 1 One top bar on every client: house, where-you-are Place, search (Standard+), and the depth chip. Replaces the Simple/Advanced toggle, the Flow header and the Workbench sidebar header.
  2. 2 Today replaces the Dashboard. Its four counters come from one server procedure (items.queue, proposal C1) so Flow, iOS and the Workbench never disagree on "what needs me".
  3. 3 Three verbs, biggest first. Act is a Standard-depth verb: at Quick depth the decision is taken on the Thing or in the Place sheet.
  4. 4 Lens bands appear only at Detail depth and only for a house that has lens data.
  5. 5 Snap keeps the Flow design. The Place default is stored server-side on the capture (C3) instead of in localStorage, so a snap from the phone is already placed on the desktop.
  6. 6 Scans, PDFs and voice are explained but not promoted; they share the same drop.
  7. 7 Sort filters are the Flow ones; Check/Place show at Standard, Lab at Detail.
  8. 8 The numbered frames are the existing Sort-frame convention (lime dashed = new, moss solid = matched).
  9. 9 Triage runs in the background after a snap (G1) when the switch in More is on, so the card is pre-filled by the time you open it. At Quick depth a row is a name only; Standard shows the kind; Detail shows attributes inline.
  10. 10 The Thing header is fixed: photo, name, kind · Place, decision badge, the five decision buttons. This is the whole Thing page at Quick depth.
  11. 11 Every other section is a disclosure: hidden when empty, collapsed by default, remembered per device. The 1548-line ItemDetail becomes a list of small section components.
  12. 12 "Add" chips are how the detailed process starts: one tap opens the section with its editor. Nothing is a separate page.
  13. 13 A lens block is a plug-in section: shown at Detail depth and only when the Thing's attributes make it relevant (a device role, a meter kind).
  14. 14 Places is the existing Focus page, promoted to a first-class tab. "Find frames" is Standard; the 2D/3D plan is Detail.
  15. 15 The thin sheet stays: name, (kind, Place at Standard), decision, Admit. Topic is no longer required to admit (A2): it defaults to the house's default kind.
  16. 16 Depth, lenses, views and AI switches live in one place and are stored on the server (settings) so all four clients agree.
